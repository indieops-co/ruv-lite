# Scout: the shortlist shape

Scout ranks candidates; it doesn't recommend adoption. Keep it under about 250 words.

**What I looked at.** One line: the idea, PRD, repo or problem, and what you read (files, sections).

**Potential fit**

```
NOW      <Name> — <Strong | Medium | Low>
LATER    <Name> — <level> once <trigger>
NOT NEEDED  <Name>, <Name>, <Name>
```

Only technologies with a matching `use_when` signal go in NOW or LATER. NOT NEEDED lists the major families
a reader might wonder about (Ruflo, RuVector, AgentDB, RVF), so the silence isn't mistaken for an oversight.
An empty NOW row is normal and correct; write `NOW      nothing`.

**Why.** Two to four sentences tying each NOW or LATER line to something in what you looked at, e.g. "Your
weekly research step splits into 40 independent lookups followed by one synthesis."

**Next.** One line offering the next step: a full consultation on the top candidate (Advise), or a
plain-English explanation of one of them (Explain). Don't offer Apply from Scout.

No THE CALL card in Scout, because Scout doesn't decide. If the user asks "so should I?", run Advise.
